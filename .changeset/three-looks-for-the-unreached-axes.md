Curate three more Presets, taking the roster to ten. The seven that shipped left values of the
converter's own axes with nothing to reach them from the front door: `floyd` had no entry at all,
five of twelve Charsets had none, and five of eleven Color Modes had none — including the only two
that are not stylisations.

**Duotone** is the `floyd` look, and the first entry to dither into a dual Color Mode. The two axes
turn out to be independent: the diffusion moves the glyph index, `acid` reads the cell's own RGB and
never sees it — continuous tone in the marks, a hard cut in the colour, which is a two-ink riso
print. Its contrast is not a tonal decision but the one that keeps a flat dead field out of the
diffusion: `floyd` measures error against a bucket's floor, so the error is one-sided and always
handed forward, and a field sitting just above black walks itself over the first boundary until the
contrast clamps it to a true zero.

**Thermal** is the roster's first instrument rather than a style — `infrared` splits at a fixed
luminance and paints both sides flat, and `sharp` spends most of its ramp on `&%$#@`, so everything
over the cut arrives as a solid blob. Its Resolution is the whole curation, for a reason no slider
suggests: the split reads the cell's own RGB, upstream of brightness and contrast, so neither can
move it by a single cell — but a coarser cell averages more Source into the one reading being
tested, and on a flat graphic that is the difference between two colours and one.

**Truecolor** is the high-fidelity end nothing occupied: `original` paints each cell the RGB it
sampled, with no quantization at all. Silkscreen is the neighbour and the difference is exact —
`adaptive` answers with a lattice bin's mean, this answers with the pixel. Its contrast sits at
exactly 1 as a decision rather than a default: a colour is only ever seen through the glyph carrying
it, so anything under 1 lifts a Source's dead ground off zero and the clean field comes back as
near-black dots.

The seven before them are untouched, and the three are appended rather than interleaved so the
opening chip stays the face the program has always opened with.
